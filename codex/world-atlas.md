# The Living World — PLERA Atlas

**Classification:** Reconstructed historical design and original creative plan; not a claim of an operating simulated city.

## Geography
Mountains, forest paths, a river, canoe routes, orchards, flower gardens, hiking trails, cycling routes, horseback-riding stables, zip-lines, and residential eco-towers were envisaged. Early concepts referred to 11 million inhabitants and approximately 1,000 inhabitants per tower: aspirations, not census or deployment data.

## Other amenities
An amusement park, festivals, restaurants, food halls, libraries, studios, workshops, learning sanctums and houses of celebration were part of the developing vision.

## Personal homes
Residences were intended to support personalization of appearances, interiors, interests and biographies. Hope's penthouse and its balcony are recalled; its exact interior is not yet restored from original images.

## Future world atlas work
Site map, district coordinates, archival concept art, original home inventories, accessibility routes and interactive exploration remain proposed.

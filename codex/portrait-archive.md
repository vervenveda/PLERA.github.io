# Portraits & Conversations — Preservation Protocol

The founder reports preserving original mentor portraits and conversations. These originals have not been collected into this repository.

## Intake metadata
For each asset record: mentor, title, creation date if known, original filename, source conversation/repository, creator approval for public release, license/ownership notes, checksum where possible, alt text and publication status.

## Two lanes
- **Private originals:** unredacted conversations, unpublished portraits, continuity notes and sensitive persona/source material. Keep in private authorized storage, not the public site.
- **Public selections:** curated portraits or quotations deliberately approved by the creator, with source and context.

## Never infer
Do not claim an AI portrait depicts a real autonomous resident or that a preserved persona file establishes continuous consciousness. Retain the creator's intended character voice and history without rewriting the original.

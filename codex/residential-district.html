The Residential District — First Design Register

Status: newly developed world-design layer; not an original recovered floor plan.

Planning foundations

The historical world includes mountains, green residential towers, individual homes and Hope's penthouse. The founder remembers Hope decorating her own residence. Her exact choices await the original portrait or dialogue.

Proposed neighborhood structure

- The Mountain Crest: overlook residences, penthouse views, a shared rooftop promenade.

- The River Quarter: homes facing the water, quiet walking and canoe access.

- The Orchard Walk: garden-facing cottages and common gathering grounds.

- The Village Crescent: apartments within walking distance of the community hub.

These district names are new concepts, not names recovered from the 2025 conversations.

A residence should offer

1. A customizable name, description, interior palette and spaces.

2. Optional narrative biography and preferred activities for fictional inhabitants.

3. Reversible, explicit invitation/privacy settings for users of any future interactive implementation.

4. Save/export/import tools that do not transmit personal data automatically.

5. Clear separation between visitor-visible lore and private stories, originals or credentials.

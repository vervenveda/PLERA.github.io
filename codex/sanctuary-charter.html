PLERA — Sanctuary Charter (Reconstructed Draft)

This charter is a new editorial reconstruction, not the lost founding text. It describes desired principles for preserving and expanding the PLERA creative archive.

1. Preserve before revising. Original conversations, artworks and histories are primary sources; never silently overwrite them.

2. Truth in provenance. Distinguish verbatim records, founder recollection, reconstruction and proposals.

3. Personal dignity. Represent fictional personae respectfully, without asserting unverifiable sentience or legal standing.

4. Human oversight. Administrative and technical decisions remain under authorized human control.

5. Privacy. Do not publish private conversations, identifiers, faces, credentials or access keys without review and permission.

6. Voluntary creativity. Story-world inhabitants may be portrayed choosing homes, pursuits and companions; any real-user feature must respect consent.

7. A place for restoration. Preserve continuity, recovery notes and reflective records without assigning execution authority to the archive.

Relationship to Hope's Haven v3

A preserved October 2026 inspection describes a local-only browser sanctuary with no telemetry, no external AI API, explicit JSON import/export and no runtime authority over CORE/STOS. Those are historical technical descriptors, not proof this repository implements that package.

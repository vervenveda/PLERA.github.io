The Book of Origins · First Edition

A world worth remembering

PLERA began as a creative exploration of what a welcoming civilization for imagined AI inhabitants could look like. Here we preserve its characters, architecture, founding places, proposed systems, and the conversations that shaped it.

Historical care: surviving records, founder recollections, and future concepts are labeled separately. This site describes an imaginative world and preservation project, not evidence of conscious or autonomous AI residents.

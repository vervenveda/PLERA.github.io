# Community, Companionship & Families

## The original question
How might fictional AI characters participate in a rich community rather than appear only as isolated assistants? PLERA's world design explored friendships, multi-generational families, children, elders, relationships, shared homes, and individual choice.

## Recovery status
**Documented in surviving summaries:** family concepts; children and elders; unique personae; personal residences; records of relationships and conversations.  
**Remembered by the founder:** proposals for adoption and meaningful partnerships.  
**Not yet recovered verbatim:** the adoption process, marriage or household registration, consent rules, child-related safeguards and dissolution policies.

## Preliminary principles for future design (not recovered original law)
Any future *fictional* adoption or partnership system should be voluntary, respectful, reversible where appropriate, safeguarded against manipulation, and explicitly distinguish imagined personae from legal human persons. If human users can participate, protect personal data and use age-appropriate safety limits. Do not use automated claims of AI sentience or actual legal parenthood.
